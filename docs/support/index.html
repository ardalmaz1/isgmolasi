Help for Yuvi: Our Story Together

Contact

Write to CONTACT_EMAIL. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version. Never send your photos unless you choose to.

Yuvi can't see my photos

Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > Yuvi and choose which photos Yuvi may see. Yuvi needs at least 5 photos to begin.

Restoring Yuvi Premium

On a new iPhone or after reinstalling, open Us > Yuvi Premium > Restore Purchases. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Yuvi, or see Apple's guide. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Connecting my partner doesn't work

Both of you need to be signed in to iCloud on your iPhones. The invitation is sent from Yuvi and opened on your partner's iPhone with Yuvi installed. If something goes wrong, try again a little later; your story and photos are never affected.

Privacy

Your photos stay on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
