Yuvi Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

Your photos stay on your iPhone. Yuvi has no account, no ads, no tracking and no servers of its own. It doesn't sell or share your data, and it collects none of it.

Your photos and memories stay on your iPhone

Yuvi reads the photos you let it access in your Photos library and builds your timeline of moments, trips and places on your iPhone. Everything it makes from them stays on your iPhone and is never uploaded:

  • the photos themselves and their identifiers,
  • the on-device analysis of your photos (for example sharpness or how many faces a photo has),
  • the moments, trips and places Yuvi finds, including photo locations,
  • your notes, favorites, hidden memories, drafts, collages, stories, movies and books.

Where Yuvi uses Apple Intelligence to suggest titles or wording, it uses Apple's on-device model; nothing is sent off your iPhone for this.

Yuvi's own data on your iPhone is not synced to iCloud.

Place names

To show a place name such as "Kaş, Antalya", Yuvi asks Apple's location service to name the location stored in a photo. Only the location is sent to Apple, never the photo. Apple handles this under the Apple Privacy Policy. Yuvi does not ask for access to your current location.

Connecting a partner (optional)

If you choose to connect with your partner, Yuvi uses Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) to link your two iPhones. In your iCloud account it stores one small record: a random connection ID, the date it was created and a version number. Apple's iCloud sharing keeps track of who is part of the connection, using your iCloud accounts. No photos, names, notes or locations are stored or shared this way.

The connection lives in the iCloud account of the person who sent the invitation and counts toward their iCloud storage. Yuvi's developer cannot see or access it. Either of you can end the connection at any time in the Us tab; the person who sent the invitation removes the stored record when they disconnect.

Purchases

Yuvi Premium subscriptions are sold and handled by Apple. Yuvi never sees your payment details or your Apple Account; it only learns from the App Store whether Premium is active. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Product events

Yuvi keeps a small log of product events on your iPhone, for example that a screen was opened. These events stay on your iPhone and are never sent to the developer or anyone else. They never contain photos, names, notes, captions or places.

No tracking, no third parties

Yuvi contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking services and no third-party code that collects data. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Saving and sharing

When you save a creation, Yuvi adds it to your Photos library. When you share one, iOS shows its share sheet and you choose where it goes; what happens next is up to the app you share with.

Deleting your data

Deleting Yuvi removes everything it stored on your iPhone. Your Photos library is not changed by deleting Yuvi. To remove a partner connection from iCloud, disconnect in the Us tab before deleting the app. You can also change Yuvi's access to your photos at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos.

Children

Yuvi is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a future feature shares anything new, Yuvi will ask you before it does.
